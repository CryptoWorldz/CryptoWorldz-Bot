# Model-348 V8 Release Controls

This release introduces the responsible V8 reward, recognition and owner-evidence architecture.

## Active first-week limits

- 3,500 Legend Point active weekly cap.
- 5,000 Legend Point absolute weekly ceiling.
- 1,500 LP Raaiiidd pool.
- 1,000 LP referral pool.
- 1,000 LP Special Request pool.
- 1,500 LP protected owner reserve, locked by default.

## Member rewards

- Qualified referral: 20 LP to the referrer and 10 LP to the new Legend.
- Shill Boost: 20 LP to a qualified newcomer after their first verified Raaiiidd.
- Verified Raaiiidd: 20 LP.
- Special Request tiers: 20, 50 or 100 LP with per-member weekly limits.
- Unique Legend: 250 LP after eligibility checks, owner review and deliberate reserve unlock.

## Recognition safeguards

Token holding and whale recognition definitions are planning records only. They award no points or payouts and remain inactive pending legal review, official-token pricing and fair public-wallet verification.

Legend Points cannot be purchased through token buys, holdings or donation amounts.

## Wallet controls

- AUD $100 Reward Wallet operating target.
- AUD $200 hard weekly deposit-recording ceiling per project wallet.
- Automatic transfers and payouts remain disabled.
- No seed phrase, private key or signing authority is stored in GitHub, Supabase or Telegram.

## Verification gate

Merge only after the complete `CryptoWorldz Zed Bot Checks` workflow runs successfully. A hosted-runner acquisition failure is an infrastructure cancellation, not a passing code result, and must be rerun before merge.
