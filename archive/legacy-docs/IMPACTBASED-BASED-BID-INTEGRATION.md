ImpactBased™ is the only active board identity.

## Current integration flow

1. A project is reviewed through ImpactBased™.
2. The approved launch destination and terms are recorded.
3. The Based.bid launch page is prepared through the verified interface.
4. The official contract address, chain, pair and public links are independently verified.
5. The token is added to `ecosystem_tokens` with an accurate launch status.
6. CryptoWorldz and the matching blockchain World display the verified record.
7. Zed may create a community mission after owner or authorised-admin review.
8. Auto remains disabled for that token unless separately allowlisted under its safety rules.

Always distinguish between project preparation, verified launch status and live market status. A pipeline listing is not a live-token claim. External contract addresses and launch destinations must be independently verified before publication.
