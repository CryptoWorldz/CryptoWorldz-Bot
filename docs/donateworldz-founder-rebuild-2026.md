## Meet JayJay — Why DonateWorldz Exists

You don't have to be wealthy to make a difference.

I'm JayJay, the person behind DonateWorldz and the Purple Diamond Crew.

Over the past four years, I've personally contributed more than AUD $5,000 to help others, even while surviving on a disability support pension and experiencing homelessness myself.

I didn't start this because I had money to spare. I started because I saw people helping others with almost nothing, and I wanted to help them keep going.

Now I want to build something bigger than myself.

A place where ordinary people everywhere can come together to support the people already making a difference — the volunteers, pastors, carers, growers, community organisers and everyday heroes working on the ground.

You don't need to be rich. You don't even need to donate money.

**Give what you can. Do what you can. Help where you can.**

Whether it's $5, a bag of seeds, a useful connection, a few hours of your time, or simply sharing someone's story, you can be part of something meaningful.

**Welcome to DonateWorldz and the Purple Diamond Crew.**

## Website structure

- Homepage: mission, JayJay story, one current verified mission, Donate / Volunteer / Contribute Supplies / Share CTAs.
- Purple Diamond Crew: real on-the-ground helpers and support roles, safeguarding, applications and contact.
- Community Impact: preserve 35 existing Facebook destinations but distinguish community links from verified recipients.
- Campaigns: individual needs, budgets, delivery partners, recipient permission, progress and receipts summary.
- Transparency: legal/operator identity, payment ownership, fees, eligibility, payout procedures, evidence and financial updates.
- Other Worldz/crypto projects: keep separate from humanitarian donation checkout and fundraising claims.
