# OneWorldz Top Up Gift Card™ System

Status: **PLANNED — DUBBO PILOT DESIGN**
Date incorporated into WorldzFullBuild™: **2026-09-27**
Owner: **JayJayTeamDev**

## Mission

**No World Hunger Ever Again.**

This workstream turns the OneWorldz mission into a measurable food-security system. OneWorldz delivers humanitarian support; DonateWorldz raises and accounts for humanitarian funding; CryptoWorldz and WorldzLaunchPad™ may generate separate commercial/technology revenue that can be deliberately routed into OneWorldz Impact.

Receiving food assistance must never require a person to buy cryptocurrency, hold a token, connect a wallet, join a crypto project, or accept investment risk.

## OneWorldz Top Up Gift Card™

Working pilot model:

- low-income / financial-hardship application or approved referral;
- initial preset value: **AUD $50**;
- food/grocery use only;
- personal recipient card or voucher;
- no cash withdrawal;
- no person-to-person transfer;
- recipient privacy preserved;
- future top-ups allowed when eligibility and funding permit;
- every funded top-up is auditable as an impact unit without publishing the recipient's identity.

### Pilot delivery rule

OneWorldz does **not** self-issue a financial product without the required legal/provider structure.

The first pilot should use an existing supermarket/food voucher or an established regulated prepaid-card/payment provider. A later persistent reloadable OneWorldz-branded card requires provider due diligence, product/legal review and clear restrictions/disclosures.

A supermarket merchant restriction alone may not guarantee "food only" because supermarkets sell non-food goods. The long-term design should prefer item-level restrictions or a partner-controlled redemption model when available.

## CryptoWorldz funding boundary

CryptoWorldz is a **technology/commercial funding engine**, not a condition of humanitarian eligibility.

Potential funding mechanisms:

- documented platform revenue;
- development services;
- advertising/sponsorship;
- education;
- crypto donations;
- WorldzLaunchPad™ commercial revenue;
- voluntary project campaigns;
- voluntary Launch for Good allocations;
- approved Worldz Fee Flow / OneWorldz Impact routes.

Humanitarian funds and commercial crypto assets must remain separately accounted for.

Charitable/community funds should not be exposed to speculative crypto trading merely to pursue returns. Crypto donations intended for humanitarian use should use an appropriate compliant process and may be converted to AUD or another stable settlement form before entering the humanitarian ledger.

## Jay's Safe Place™

Jay's Safe Place is a local community information/referral concept. It is **not** represented as an emergency service, housing provider, medical service or professional case-management service.

Purpose:

- give a person doing it tough one clear next step;
- connect people to existing Dubbo services;
- make food, housing, mental-health and community-support information easier to find;
- support existing organisations rather than replace them.

### Business card — front concept

Primary elements:

- possible copy:
  - Need Food?
  - Need Somewhere Safe to Sit?

- **Helping the People who Help the People**
- OneWorldz;
- Jason / Jay as community contact where appropriate;
- QR to a live Jay's Safe Place / OneWorldz support page;

- **Housing & Homelessness** — verified local housing/homelessness pathways;
- **Community** — Miracle Church Dubbo and other verified community organisations;
- **Emergency** — 000 for immediate danger/emergency assistance;
- QR to live service information.

All service names, addresses, schedules, eligibility details and contact data must be verified before printing.

## Live support directory principle

Printed materials should point to a maintainable live page so changing service information can be updated without reprinting every card/pamphlet.

Target live fields:

- service name;
- service category;
- address;
- hours;
- meal schedule;
- eligibility;
- phone/contact;
- accessibility notes;
- transport/bus information;
- last verified date.

No stale service information should be knowingly published.

## Measurement

Minimum impact metrics:

- dollars raised;
- dollars settled into humanitarian fund;
- number of $50 top-ups funded;
- number issued;
- number redeemed;
- unused/expired value;
- infrastructure items funded;
- kilograms/meals/food-support units where partner data can support it;
- partner locations;
- repeat assistance;
- funding source split;
- administrative/processing costs;
- verified CryptoWorldz-to-OneWorldz contributions;
- recipient identity kept private in public reporting.

## Regression-prevention / integrity rules

- Never require crypto participation for food.
- Never describe a proposed HopeStreet/BaptistCare relationship as confirmed.
- Never advertise a grant as secured until awarded.
- Never call the OneWorldz card live until a real provider/retailer and end-to-end redemption path are verified.
- Never claim "food only" unless the restriction is technically/operationally true.
- Never use recipient identities in public impact reporting without explicit lawful consent.
- Never hold or speculate with humanitarian money in crypto merely to chase profit.
- Never fabricate service schedules, contacts, partner approvals or funding.
- Never treat a token donation promise as received funding.
- Never count projected Launch for Good revenue as delivered humanitarian impact.
- Keep OneWorldz/DonateWorldz humanitarian messaging distinct from CryptoWorldz investment/technology messaging.
