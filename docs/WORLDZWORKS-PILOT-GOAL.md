# WorldzWorks™ pilot goal

Owner direction — 26 September 2026: keep one small **real revenue project** as a 100% committed ecosystem goal. Publish its actual costs, sales, worker payments and community contribution before expanding to an investment pathway.

First pilot proof should include a named operator, real product or service, public prices, receipts for costs and sales, a worker payment record shared with consent, a clearly separated community contribution, and a short monthly report. Never label projected income as achieved profit. Donations, customer purchases, worker pay and investment must each retain distinct terms and accounting. This goal does not alter REVIVE launch or distribution gates.
