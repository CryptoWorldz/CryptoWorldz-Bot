# WORLDZ Dev City — Founding 100 Charter (Draft)

**Status:** Proposed program; enrollment, token claims, funding and rewards are not yet live.

## Mission
**You Don't Need to Be a Famous Developer. You Just Need a Chance.**

Dev City exists to welcome aspiring developers into the Worldz ecosystem through guided contributions, mentoring, learning and fair, verifiable rewards.

## First intake: 100 positions
- **60** new and aspiring developers (highlight group; no prior token launch required)
- **25** intermediate developers
- **15** experienced developers, reviewers and mentors
- Reserve **150 additional positions** for later expansion, subject to program review.

## Ownership and control
- Founder retains strategic direction and approval of critical changes.
- No membership grants equity, ownership, admin access, treasury signing power, production credentials or token mint authority.
- Contributors use scoped issues, forks/branches and pull requests, with review and CI before merge.
- No private keys, seed phrases or secrets in applications or pull requests.
- Publish contributor agreements, licensing and intellectual-property terms before accepting external code.
- Security-sensitive changes require independent review and explicit approval.

## Beginner-first participation
- Starter issues: documentation, accessibility, UI fixes, testing, translations and reproducible bug reports.
- Mentored projects: wallet-connect test harnesses, launch previews, proof explorers, fee disclosures and chain testnet integration.
- Offer learning materials, feedback and non-token recognition even when bounties are unavailable.
- No requirement to hold WORLDZ, pay an application fee, buy NFTs or have prior crypto launches.

## Reward eligibility
- Verification confirms one applicant and control of a public wallet, without requiring seed phrases.
- Rewards are earned only against pre-approved scopes, budgets, tests and accepted deliverables.
- Record approved awards and vesting/claim receipts transparently.
- **Proposed Dev City reserve:** 10% of a **proposed** fixed 348,000,000 WORLDZ supply = 34,800,000 WORLDZ. No mint or distribution approved.
- Distinguish Dev City contribution rewards from the separate Founding 100 launch bonus program.

## Next gates
1. Founder approves charter and contributor access policy.
2. Publish beginner onboarding, mentoring and bounty rubric.
3. Establish a reviewed GitHub issue intake with no production access.
4. Audit treasury, vesting, legal and tax implications before token awards.
5. Open the first 100 positions only after application and review processes are ready.
