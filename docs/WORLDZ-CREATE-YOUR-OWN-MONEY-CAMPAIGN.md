## Public headline

> CREATE YOUR OWN MONEY‼️*

The asterisk is deliberate and must remain visible in long-form campaign surfaces.

**Truth line:** Create your own crypto token. A newly created token is not automatically legal tender, valuable, liquid or accepted by anyone.

## Objective

Make token creation understandable to ordinary people without presenting token creation as guaranteed wealth.

The campaign should make it simple enough that a family, group of friends, community builder or first-time creator can understand:

1. token name and symbol;
2. blockchain/network;
3. total supply;
4. distribution;
5. liquidity;
6. purpose/utility;
7. authority state;
8. public launch proof.

## Message pillars

### 1. Anyone can understand the basics
No developer vocabulary is required for the first lesson.

### 2. Create before you speculate
The first action is to design the idea and document the rules, not buy something.

### 3. Share it with family and friends
The campaign is built around collaborative learning and community creation.

### 4. Proof over hype
Supply, authorities, allocations, vesting, liquidity and transactions should be evidenced rather than merely claimed.

### 5. Creation does not equal guaranteed value
No Worldz surface should imply that minting a token creates guaranteed wealth, liquidity or investment returns.
