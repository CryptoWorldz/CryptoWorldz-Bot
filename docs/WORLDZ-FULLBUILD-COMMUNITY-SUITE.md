# Worldz FullBuild™ Community Suite — Product Blueprint v1

## Product
A Worldz-hosted Telegram community platform built around two specialised bot roles:
1. Operations Bot — ZED • AUTO • G.R.A.C.E. Command Centre functions, raids, missions, points, voting, scanning, alerts, settings and admin controls.
2. Custom AI Community Bot — configurable name, personality, knowledge, support and conversational assistance.

Customers can purchase either bot alone or the complete two-bot suite.

## Licence choices
- Starter Trial: 0.05 SOL / 7 days / 1 group; credited toward an upgrade.
- Rent: 0.3 SOL / group / 31 days.
- Rent-to-Own: 0.5 SOL / group / month for 12 months.
- Own: 4.5 SOL one-time perpetual group licence for the purchased release.

Third-party API charges, premium AI usage, hosting outside included limits and future major-version upgrades can be separately priced.
