The approved launch-engineering target is **5% of total supply = 17,400,000 MRCL**, with an **AUD $200 target creator first buy in SOL** and staged additions later.

For Meteora DBC, these are two different mechanics:

1. The DBC launch begins as a **virtual pool**. Virtual reserves define curve price behavior; they are not real SOL, real TVL, or doubled liquidity.
2. The AUD $200 target is implemented as a **real SOL first buy** (supported by current DBC creator-first-buy flows). That SOL enters the DBC quote reserve through the purchase.
3. When the configured graduation threshold is reached, the accumulated quote reserve plus the configured base-token amount form the initial DAMM v2 liquidity.
4. Additional owner-approved SOL/token liquidity can then be added as staged DAMM v2 positions after migration.

Do **not** create a second competing traditional AMM pool beside the DBC at genesis. That would fragment liquidity and price discovery.

The exact SOL amount is deliberately not hard-coded now. At transaction build/review time, the system binds the AUD $200 target to a live AUD/SOL quote, shows the exact SOL amount, simulates it, and requires the normal approval/signature gates.
