# WORLDZ INCORPORATED™ — Official Vision and Ecosystem Integration

Approved 24 October 2026. Canonical mission text; preserve wording.

# Helping the People Who Help the People.

Worldz is building a global network of community-first businesses, food programs, artists, farmers, recyclers, technology builders and everyday people working together to reduce hunger and hardship.

We believe affordable food should be available to everyone. We believe people who help their communities deserve practical support. We believe businesses can succeed without extracting excessive profits, and that contributions should produce visible, measurable results.

Our ambition is to connect local action with global opportunity — beginning with food, expanding into farming, employment, infrastructure and responsible financial technology.

Everyone has something to contribute. Every contribution should make a difference.

## Integration brief — proposals, not claims of launched operations

- **WorldzHQ**: canonical vision, project directory, transparent progress, partner applications.
- **DonateWorldz / Purple Diamond Crew**: verified grassroots aid, Dubbo free-food directory, recipient privacy, community contribution pathways.
- **Worldz Takeaway**: $3 target cup of hot chips, affordable classics, nutritional meal options, $5 optional pay-it-forward contributions, weekend meal pilot and supplier partnerships. Prices illustrative until costed and approved.
- **Worldz Revival**: performers can support with performances, sponsorship, meals, promotion, equipment and verified impact.
- **WorldzVillage**: community-led farming/infrastructure pilots starting with Uganda; Australian remote projects only with local and Traditional Owner agreement.
- **Worldz Recycling**: lawful, engineered reuse of tyres, metal, plastics and recovered equipment with safety/environmental checks.
- **WorldzKitty**: transparent public counters for contributions, expenses, verified meals and project results; separate restricted charitable donations from business and crypto treasuries.
- **Worldz Business Builder**: local employment, fair wages, employee ownership pathways, suppliers and donated farm equipment.
- **WorldzLaunchPad & payments**: optional compliant payment infrastructure; no promise of token returns or dependency on tokens for essential help.

## Visual design system

Primary deep royal purple; secondary electric/midnight blue; black backgrounds and silver accents. Blend an accessible deep-purple star-galaxy theme with a restrained illustrated border. Original artwork only; avoid reproducing culturally significant Aboriginal designs without a local Aboriginal artist's participation, permission and attribution. Keep all mobile content readable. Preserve existing site-specific functions, wallets, payment interfaces and legacy archive content.

## Domain release safeguards

The repository's DOMAINS.txt is a legacy list, not an authoritative current domain registry. Confirm ownership, host mapping, DNS, SSL, redirects, content roots and function tests separately for every domain. Do not change DNS, checkout flows, wallet code, critical automation, or deploy production solely because of this planning document.

## Publication rule

Distinguish clearly between active, pilot, proposed and research-stage initiatives. Do not invent money raised, meal totals, endorsements, sponsors, government partnerships or approvals.
