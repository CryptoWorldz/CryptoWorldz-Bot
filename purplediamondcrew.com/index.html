Purple Diamond Crew (PDC) is Worldz's community humanitarian initiative, focused on practical support for people facing hunger and hardship and for trusted local helpers already doing the work.

We are building a framework for real-world assistance, clear accountability and community partnerships. Proposed projects are not represented as completed or funded.

What Purple Diamond Crew does — and aims to build

Food & essentials

Support for meals, food hampers, clothing, bedding, blankets and essential supplies through verified local helpers.

Shelter & dignity

Practical assistance pathways for people without stable accommodation, working alongside appropriate community services.

Water & farming

Longer-term ambitions include clean-water access, community gardens, farming equipment, local food production and sustainable food programs.

Back the helpers

Identify and support accountable volunteers, organisations and on-the-ground teams who already serve their communities.

How PDC fits within Worldz

WorldzHQ introduces the overall mission. DonateWorldz is intended for direct humanitarian support pathways. FoodWorldz focuses on food and water. WorldzLaunchPad is a separate technology and token-launch initiative. The proposed Purple Diamond Crew Treasury is one of three distinct governance departments in the Worldz ecosystem.

Under the current proposed treasury revenue policy, the departments receive 50% Main Operations, 30% Miracle Team and 20% Purple Diamond Crew of the designated treasury lane, not of all trading or donations. The PDC multisignature wallet is not yet deployed, and production routing remains disabled pending verification. No charity status, endorsement or completed aid project is implied without supporting evidence.
