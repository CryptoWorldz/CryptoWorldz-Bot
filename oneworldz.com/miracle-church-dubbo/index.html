Fresh Water
Art Exhibition

Miracle Church Dubbo is hosting a local art exhibition celebrating water, renewal and regional life. The exhibition continues through November 2026, with artworks displayed throughout the church and works available to purchase.

📍 51 Thompson St, Dubbo 🎨 Local artists 🖼️ Artwork for purchase ♿ Accessible venue

On now in Dubbo

See the exhibition while you visit Miracle Church

Fresh Water officially opened on Saturday 26 September. The published exhibition schedule runs through 11 November from 9am–3pm, followed by Sunday-only viewing through 29 November. Entry is free. If a work catches your eye, speak with Alison at Miracle Church about purchasing the artwork or contact the church by email.

Join a Sunday service too

Miracle Church welcomes visitors to its Sunday services. The church lists services at 9am, 11am and 5:30pm. Their service information also describes time to connect over tea, coffee and something to eat, making the exhibition a natural part of a wider community visit.
