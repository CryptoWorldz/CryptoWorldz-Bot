Testnet launch configuration

Connect GemWallet on XRPL Testnet. Use a dedicated issuer account and a separate hot/operational account.

Issuer approvals

Connect GemWallet to the issuer account. These settings are separate approvals and are shown separately in proof.

No Freeze is an issuer-control choice. It is not proof of fixed supply by itself. Token escrow stays feature-gated until XRPL reports the required amendment available.

Hot-wallet trust line

Switch GemWallet to the hot wallet entered above, reconnect, then approve the trust line.

Issue supply

Switch GemWallet back to the issuer, reconnect, then issue the configured amount to the hot wallet. Worldz labels this intended supply until issuer control is separately proven immutable.

Native Token/XRP AMM

XRPWorldz Fair Fee: 1.00% default with simple 0.25% choices down to 0.25%. XRPL itself supports finer fee precision, but Worldz deliberately keeps the creator experience simple. Worldz shows the AMM's implied starting ratio. Liquidity existence is not the same as issuer immutability or LP-style lock proof.
