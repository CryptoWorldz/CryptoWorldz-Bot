WORLDZ SAFE LAUNCH STANDARD™

Creators can build. Nobody gets to switch the safety rules off.

These are platform limits, not suggested tokenomics. A creator may choose values inside the box; WorldzLaunchPad rejects manifests outside it. Mainnet remains fail-closed until the same rules are enforced on-chain.

HARD RULE 01

Fixed supply.

Mint authority revoked after genesis. Freeze authority revoked/absent. No later inflation switch.

HARD RULE 02

Founder cap.

Creator/team ≤15% total; ≤5% liquid at genesis; ≥90-day cliff and ≥18-month vesting on the remainder.

HARD RULE 03

Liquidity cannot vanish.

25–60% supply allocation to liquidity. 100% of creator-controlled LP must be locked ≥365 days for a verified mainnet launch.

HARD RULE 04

No tax trap.

0% wallet-transfer tax. Supported project trading fee 0.50–3.00%. Creator fee route ≤20%; LP-growth route ≥20%.

HARD RULE 05

Multisig control.

Treasury and any program authority must be 2-of-3 or stronger multisig before mainnet verification.

HARD RULE 06

Everything disclosed.

Tagged genesis wallets, public allocation manifest + hash, no hidden private allocations, no blacklist/sell restriction and no undisclosed private discount.
