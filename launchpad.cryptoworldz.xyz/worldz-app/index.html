MODULE REGISTRY

One app shell. Separate authority.

These modules are registered in the WorldzApp contract. An app tile never grants signing or financial authority by itself.

TREASURY PROFILES

Governance remains profile-specific.

Signer membership in one treasury does not silently make a person a signer of another treasury.

READ-ONLY CHAIN ADAPTERS

Observe first. Execute later.

Adapters read balances, multisig configuration, proposal history and transaction state without exposing signing or broadcast methods.

EXTERNAL WALLET CONNECTIONS

Your wallet comes to WorldzApp™.

Connection-only adapters may read a public address and network after approval. Stage 3B adds a guarded bridge for XRPL/Sui provider integrations and mobile handoff preparation without enabling transaction signatures.

WORLDZ IDENTITY + MOBILE HANDOFF

Roles are verified. Authority stays separate.

Self-asserted public identity cannot become a privileged Worldz role. Team, signer, operator and admin assertions require a trusted verified issuer, and even a verified role does not bypass multisig or chain release gates.

MOBILE CONNECTION FOUNDATION

Fail closed until provider configuration is verified.

WalletConnect pairing, Xaman OAuth and Sui dApp Kit are represented by guarded connection-only contracts. Pairing and OAuth credentials must remain memory-only and never enter the public Worldz session.

SIMULATION

Stage 4 starts with human-readable effects.

MULTISIG APPROVAL EVIDENCE

Thresholds can be proven without giving WorldzApp the keys.

Stage 5 normalizes public approval state from Squads, Safe, XRPL and Sui. Reaching threshold is evidence only; execution and broadcast remain separate gates.

CAPABILITY FIREWALL

Deny unless explicitly enabled.

WorldzApp core coordinates modules without becoming a master key. Mainnet actions remain gated at the module, treasury and blockchain level.

NON-NEGOTIABLE

WorldzApp never asks for:

  • Seed phrase
  • Private key
  • Recovery words
  • A universal key shared across blockchains

Wallet signing stays with the connected wallet, hardware device or native multisig system.
