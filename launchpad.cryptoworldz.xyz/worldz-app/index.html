MODULE REGISTRY

One app shell. Separate authority.

These modules are registered in the WorldzApp contract. An app tile never grants signing or financial authority by itself.

TREASURY PROFILES

Governance remains profile-specific.

Signer membership in one treasury does not silently make a person a signer of another treasury.

READ-ONLY CHAIN ADAPTERS

Observe first. Execute later.

These adapters define how WorldzApp will read balances, multisig configuration, proposal history and transaction state without exposing any signing or broadcast method.

PUBLIC IDENTITY + SESSION

AUTHORITY RULE

Connected ≠ Verified ≠ Treasury Signer

A connected public address remains unverified until a future challenge proof succeeds. Even a verified wallet does not become a treasury signer unless the treasury-specific consent and chain-native multisig enrollment gates pass.

EXTERNAL SELF-CUSTODY CONNECTIONS

Bring the wallet to WorldzApp™.

Connection requests ask the external wallet for public account/network metadata only. Stage 3 contains no transaction-signing or broadcast implementation.
