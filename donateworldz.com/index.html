Clarity before contributions

Each recipient and purpose must be clearly identified. A contribution to a project builder is not a donation to a family. Crypto activities are separate from humanitarian support.

We do not claim every cause is independently verified or promise tax deductibility. Check each recipient's information and payment terms before contributing. This site does not collect payment card details.
