# WORLDZ VOTES CENTRE™ — Civic Charter

**Worldz 🌐 A Better World 🌏 💜🌈⭐🎢**

## Intention

Worldz Votes Centre™ exists to make public participation easier to understand, fairer to access, calmer to use, transparent to audit and safer for participants.

Worldz does **not** decide political outcomes for people. It provides an equal voting surface so people can examine proposals, compare evidence and record their own preferences.

## Non-negotiable principles

1. **Equal voting power** — one verified eligible person gets one vote in a ballot.
2. **Equal treatment** — candidates/options use the same presentation rules, character limits and ballot controls.
3. **No paid ballot advantage** — sponsored placement, paid ranking, boosted ballot position and purchased votes are prohibited.
4. **Clear rules before voting** — method, eligibility, opening time, closing time, counting rules and tie rules are published before a ballot opens.
5. **Secret-ballot target** — identity/eligibility systems must be separated from stored vote choices. Binding use requires independent privacy/security review.
6. **Transparent count** — publish the counting method, totals, exhausted ballots where relevant, audit receipts and corrections.
7. **Evidence before slogans** — factual claims should link to sources. Materially disputed claims should identify disagreement rather than present opinion as fact.
8. **Neutral platform** — Worldz does not endorse parties, candidates or referendum positions.
9. **Lawful operation** — every jurisdiction gets a dated compliance profile. Current law controls until changed through lawful democratic processes.
10. **No false authority** — Worldz public consultations are non-binding unless the relevant legal authority formally authorises a binding integration.
11. **Accessibility** — plain language, accessible interfaces and multilingual support should be designed in from the start.
12. **Safety** — minimise identity collection, never publish individual ballots, protect participants from harassment and publish security/audit findings.

## Product separation

Worldz Votes Centre™ has distinct namespaces:

- **Token Popularity** — existing DEX-style hourly favourite-token voting.
- **Civic Public Voice** — public-policy/civic consultation.
- **WorldzGovern™** — separate DAO/governance execution. Civic votes never move funds or execute treasury/governance actions.

## Civic ballot lifecycle

DRAFT → SOURCED → LEGAL REVIEW → PUBLIC PREVIEW → OPEN → CLOSED → COUNTED → AUDITED → ARCHIVED

A ballot cannot become binding merely because a Worldz administrator changes a flag. Binding use requires an external official-authority reference plus legal, identity, privacy and independent audit gates.

## Equal presentation standard

Every candidate/option receives:
- the same card size and information fields;
- the same maximum description length;
- the same evidence/source fields;
- the same placement logic;
- no paid boost;
- no hidden weighting.

Option order should be rotated or randomised using a published method when legally appropriate.

## Funding transparency

Worldz should publish funding and electoral-spend compliance information when applicable. Product policy is stricter than many current laws: **money cannot buy a better position inside the ballot interface.**

## Australian federal compliance snapshot

This repository includes a dated Australia federal compliance profile. It is a technical guardrail, not legal advice.

As at 2026-10-02:
- certain federal electoral communications can require authorisation under the Commonwealth Electoral Act 1918;
- transitional funding/disclosure rules operate during 2026;
- major Electoral Reform Act 2025 funding/disclosure changes commence 2027-01-01.

The profile must be reviewed whenever the AEC rules change.

## Build gate

**Binding civic voting remains OFF by default.**

The non-binding public consultation layer can be piloted while the following production gates are completed:
- jurisdiction legal review;
- strong eligibility verification;
- privacy-preserving credential design;
- independent security review;
- independent election-method audit;
- accessibility review;
- incident response and recount procedure.

Worldz aims to help people participate — not tell them what to choose.
