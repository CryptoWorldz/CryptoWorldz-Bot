Custody and operational controls

Developer wallet control

Worldz operational and treasury funds use a developer-wallet-only policy, subject to wallet ownership and transaction proof before any routing is enabled.

Separate accounting

Humanitarian donations, business revenue, token liquidity and developer operating funds must remain distinguishable, with authorised records and receipts.

Verification before release

Wallet addresses, allocations, receiving authority, transaction signing, liquidity permissions and publication require independent validation. Mainnet launch gates remain closed until proven.

Mission and funding lanes

Worldz Incorporated supports technology and operations; Purple Diamond Crew focuses on practical humanitarian action; and the Miracle Team remains an independently described community initiative. Historical revenue splits are proposals, not proof of active financial transfers. Any final allocation must be reconciled with a single published policy and verified receipts.

No humanitarian support requires purchasing a token. DonateWorldz donations and their impact records must remain separate from trading and speculative activity.
