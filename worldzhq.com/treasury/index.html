What is a multisignature treasury?

A multisig wallet requires several independent approvals before funds can move. The planned Main Treasury uses 3 of 5 signers; the Miracle Team aims for 4 of 7. Membership does not automatically grant signing power. Spending proposals, signers, approvals, reporting, withdrawal and exit rights need written rules.

Indicative wallet creation allowance: approximately A$25 per multisig treasury wallet, or around A$75 for three, subject to actual provider, network and setup costs. This is an estimate, not a verified fixed fee. The founder's replacement developer wallet and final signer arrangements also remain to be completed.

Founding 20: equal proposed personal allocations

Each eligible founding member is proposed to receive 0.5% of the final total WORLDZ token supply into their own wallet, whether their departmental starting contribution is A$100, A$150 or A$200. Twenty such allocations would total 10% of supply. These are proposed allocations, not issued tokens or promises of value; they require final tokenomics, legal review and an approved agreement.

Personal holdings would be separate from treasury assets. Holding or adding to a token position is a personal choice, not a savings account or guaranteed investment. Tokens can lose all value.

How initial Solana liquidity could work

If 20 people each contributed A$100, the total would be A$2,000. If an approved treasury used A$2,000 of SOL alongside A$2,000 worth of WORLDZ tokens at the initial pool price, that could form an illustrative A$4,000 WORLDZ/SOL liquidity pool. The WORLDZ side is tokens, not another A$2,000 of cash.

Liquidity makes swaps possible and deeper liquidity generally reduces price impact, but a small pool remains volatile. Providers face token losses, impermanent loss, technical risks and potentially total loss. Contributions are not accepted while the legal and ownership arrangements remain unresolved.

How WorldzLaunchPad can support the mission

WorldzLaunchPad is designed to help creators launch tokens with disclosed fees and safer-launch controls. Under the existing fee policy, eligible creator fee revenue may contribute 3%, 5% or 8% to Worldz, depending on the creator-selected profile. Of that Worldz contribution, the treasury lane is 20%, with a newer departmental plan of 50% Main, 30% Miracle Team and 20% Purple Diamond Crew. These are percentages of defined revenue lanes, not percentages of trading volume.

Separate platform policies also propose a disclosed 0.60% genesis token-supply allocation on applicable launches (0.30% Main, 0.18% Miracle Team, 0.12% PDC). These are separate from cash fee revenue and from personal Founding 20 allocations. Actual routing remains gated pending technical verification.

Humanitarian funding and member-investment assets must be legally and financially separated. Neither trading revenue nor charitable impact is guaranteed.

OPEN DEVELOPMENT • WORLDZ BUILD ROADMAP

What @JayJayTeamDev is building

Worldz is more than a proposed cryptocurrency. It is an evolving ecosystem linking transparent technology, local businesses, food programs, employment opportunities and practical community support. The aim is for successful businesses and technology services to help sustain the wider mission — Helping the People Who Help the People. We publish both the ambition and its current stage so people can judge the work for themselves.

📱 WorldzApp™ — foundation built, release pending

A planned single entry point for WorldzHQ, launchpad tools, wallet connections, treasury proposals, proofs, community modules and future humanitarian services. GitHub contains an installable web-app foundation, module registry and Android packaging preparation. A signed, publicly released Android application is not yet completed.

👛 Worldz Wallet™ — in design

The goal is a user-controlled wallet experience, starting with secure wallet connection and clear transaction review, then developing chain-specific capabilities. The existing app has Solana/EVM connection foundations and guarded simulations; it does not yet claim a released native wallet, automated swaps, or approved mainnet money movement. We will not ask for private keys or recovery phrases.

🪙 $WORLDZ — Solana first, XRP next

A new WORLDZ token is being designed with one publicly reconciled total supply across participating networks, rather than pretending separate unrestricted mints are one coin. Initial launch and liquidity are planned on Solana. Next is an XRP integration path: verified wXRP liquidity routes on Solana, XRPL EVM research, and a separately tested native XRP Ledger token and liquidity market. No cross-chain transfer route or native XRPL mainnet launch is represented as live.

🌉 Multiblockchain expansion — research and staged testing

Future networks under consideration include Base, Ethereum, Bitcoin-related routes, Hyper, Sui and Robinhood-linked chain infrastructure, with other networks researched later. Bridge or interoperability approaches such as LayerZero's OFT/adapter model must pass security, supply-reconciliation, liquidity and chain-compatibility checks. Every chain would need its own genuine trading liquidity and approved deployment.

🚀 WorldzLaunchPad — under active development

Designed for more understandable token launches, fee disclosure, liquidity planning, creator tools, wallet research and launch safety checks. The repository includes Solana-oriented web tools and an XRPL testnet adapter. Integrations, live routing and production permissions are deliberately gated until tested. Revenue could support operations, Miracle Team and Purple Diamond Crew under published rules, but no revenue is guaranteed.

🍟 Worldz Takeaways & future franchises — business proposals

Affordable old-school takeaway food, including a target A$3 cup of chips, practical meals and optional pay-it-forward contributions toward meals for people in need. These are ideas under costing and local planning, not currently operating outlets or advertised franchises. The longer-term goal is repeatable, locally run businesses that create jobs, pay suppliers fairly and fund measurable community assistance.

How the wider ecosystem can create useful business

WorldzHQ is the public directory and accountability hub. FoodWorldz and proposed takeaways focus on affordable food; DonateWorldz and Purple Diamond Crew focus on practical humanitarian support; WorldzLaunchPad, WorldzApp and the proposed Worldz Wallet form the technology pathway. Later opportunities being explored include farming and community food production, waste recycling, local supply chains, payment and remittance services, business partnerships, cultural events and Worldz franchises.

The proposed cycle is straightforward: build useful services → attract customers and partners → account for legitimate revenue and costs → reinvest in jobs, business growth and approved humanitarian work → publish evidence of outcomes. Crypto trading is optional; nobody should need to buy WORLDZ to receive essential help.

Where the project stands today

Some software foundations, interfaces and testnet paths already exist in GitHub. The new $WORLDZ token issuance, initial Solana liquidity, XRPL integration, cross-chain deployment, Worldz Wallet release, operating takeaways and franchise program remain subject to design, funding, security checks, approvals and testing.
