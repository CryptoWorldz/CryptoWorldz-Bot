Build it together, responsibly

1. Community first

Invite musicians, volunteers, local organizations and supporters to express interest.

2. Check feasibility

Investigate venue availability, accessibility, permissions, safety, insurance and realistic costs.

3. Partners and artists

Approach sponsors and performers. An artist wish list is not a confirmed lineup.

4. Confirm before selling

Publish verified announcements and only offer tickets once the event is financially and operationally viable.

Why we're doing this

Worldz is building a community of people helping people. The festival vision is to connect music and practical support for humanitarian projects, with transparent reporting on any future fundraising.

Local performers, tribute acts and volunteers are welcome in the planning conversation. No artist, sponsor, venue or charity partnership is being represented as confirmed.
