# WORLDZ GLOBAL PUBLIC VOICE™ 🌐

**Worldz 🌐 A Better World 🌍**

Worldz Public Voice is worldwide by design.

A person should not be excluded from the non-binding Worldz public-voice layer because they live in Uganda, elsewhere in Africa, Indonesia, the Philippines, Greenland, Iceland, Mexico, Austria, Belgium, France, Hong Kong, China, India, Australia, or anywhere else.

Those examples are not an allowlist. **There is no country or territory allowlist for the general non-binding Worldz public-voice layer.**

## Equal voice

Worldz does not give extra civic weight because of:
- location or nationality;
- race or ethnicity;
- wealth;
- token holdings;
- social status.

General public-voice participation is designed to be age-inclusive. Children and young people require age-appropriate privacy and safety protections. Any official or legally binding vote must still follow the lawful eligibility rules of the relevant jurisdiction and cannot be self-authorised by Worldz.

Worldz should not require a person's race, ethnicity or exact home address to let them document a public concern.

## Human-needs mission

Worldz works toward a world where **no child or adult dies from hunger or is denied available preventable care because money stood in the way**.

Worldz Public Voice can document concerns and priorities about:
- food and hunger;
- preventable disease;
- essential healthcare and medicines;
- clean water and sanitation;
- safe shelter and housing;
- education and opportunity;
- how public and community resources are prioritised.

This is a public-voice and transparency mission. Worldz does not claim legal authority over government budgets and civic votes do not execute Worldz treasury actions.

## Public money

People should be able to document where they believe public and community resources should go, compare proposals and see aggregated priorities transparently.

Worldz can record and publish those preferences without pretending a non-binding consultation is an official budget decision.

## Political neutrality

Worldz may document support, opposition, concerns, evidence and public results. It does not tell a person which politician, party or referendum position to choose.

Criticism of public figures can be documented as public opinion where lawful, but the platform-wide product identity remains neutral rather than becoming a campaign for or against a named politician.

## Worldwide legal architecture

There is no single Worldz rule that replaces local law.

- **Worldwide layer:** open non-binding public voice, equal presentation, concern documentation and transparent aggregation.
- **Jurisdiction adapter:** dated local legal/privacy/election requirements when regulated activity is involved.
- **Binding/official mode:** OFF unless lawful authority, eligibility, privacy and independent audit gates are satisfied.

Australia is one worked compliance profile, not the geographic boundary of Worldz.


## Build #2 — read-only worldwide discovery

The worldwide Public Voice foundation now has a usable read-only discovery layer:

- `GET /api/worldz-votes/civic/priorities` publishes the human-needs/resource-priority taxonomy as non-binding public priorities.
- `GET /api/worldz-votes/civic/concerns` exposes only concerns already marked `published`.
- concern discovery can be filtered by approved topic, location scope, country/territory code and language code.
- the MiniApp renders the priority categories and published concern cards from the live API.
- public concern submission remains OFF until moderation, privacy, abuse-prevention and age-appropriate safety gates are implemented and reviewed.
- binding/official voting remains OFF unless lawful authority and the jurisdiction-specific legal, identity, privacy and independent-audit gates are satisfied.

This layer is for transparent public expression and discovery. It does not rank politicians or parties, decide elections, control government budgets, or execute Worldz treasury transactions.
