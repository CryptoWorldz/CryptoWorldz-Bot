# WorldzApp™ Privacy Policy — Pre-Submission Draft

**Status: DRAFT — NOT YET THE FINAL PUBLIC PLAY STORE POLICY**  
Last updated: 27 September 2026

WorldzApp™ is designed as a non-custodial gateway to the WorldzEcosystem™.

## Wallet information

When a user chooses to connect a supported external wallet, WorldzApp may read public connection information such as the public wallet address, wallet/provider label and selected blockchain network. The current WorldzApp core keeps this information in browser session storage by default. Persistence of a public wallet address beyond the session requires explicit user consent under the WorldzApp storage policy.

WorldzApp does not ask users to provide seed phrases, private keys, recovery phrases, wallet unlock passwords or exported signing keys.

## Treasury and proof information

WorldzApp may work with public blockchain and treasury-coordination information such as public treasury identifiers, proposal identifiers, public approval-state references, transaction hashes and WorldzProof™ receipts. Blockchain data that is public on a distributed ledger remains publicly observable independently of WorldzApp.

## Identity and roles

The current public core does not create a Worldz-hosted consumer account. If a future submitted release introduces Worldz-hosted account creation, the privacy policy and Google Play declarations must be updated before that feature ships, including account-deletion controls where required.

## Third-party wallets and providers

Wallets, blockchain RPC providers and other external services have their own privacy practices. Connecting to or opening an external provider may cause information to be handled by that provider under its own terms and privacy policy.

## Security design

WorldzApp separates public identity, chain reads, simulation, multisig evidence and transaction authority. The Android package does not itself enable mainnet broadcasting. Capabilities are released chain by chain and module by module.

## Data retention and deletion

A final production retention and deletion statement will be completed after the exact production hosting, analytics, support and identity configuration is audited. WorldzApp will not claim a deletion practice in Google Play until that practice is implemented and verified.
