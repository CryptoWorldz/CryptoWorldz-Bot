# OneWorldz Head Domain V2 — State-of-the-Art Master Plan

Authority: JayJayTeamDev approved OneWorldz.com head-domain redesign and coordinated Learn/Law/ImpactBased upgrade on 15 August 2026.

This plan supersedes the prior `OneWorldz unchanged` rule only for the explicitly reviewed OneWorldz V2 release. It does not remove the protected status of CryptoBotz, DNS, mail, payment credentials, owner controls or unrelated services.

## Mission

OneWorldz.com becomes the unmistakable global front door to the ecosystem: cinematic, human, premium, fast, clear and emotionally memorable. It must make a first-time visitor immediately understand three things:

1. OneWorldz is about people and real-world impact.
2. The ecosystem has specialised destinations rather than duplicated websites.
3. Learn.OneWorldz, Law.OneWorldz and ImpactBased.OneWorldz are first-class divisions of the same global platform.

Primary brand line: `OneWorldz 🌐 One Vision.`
Supporting line: `One World • One Mission`
Mission line: `Helping the People Who Help People.`

## Experience Standard

- Cinematic desktop and mobile layouts using approved or newly commissioned production artwork only.
- No stretched screenshots, placeholder cards, generic stock-site appearance or mismatched image reuse.
- Large visual storytelling with restrained text; visitors should understand sections in seconds.
- Dark premium base with luminous violet, blue, gold and natural earth highlights.
- Smooth but lightweight motion; reduced-motion accessibility must be respected.
- Excellent Android/mobile fit, full-width action controls where needed, keyboard focus, readable contrast and no horizontal overflow.
- Every primary destination is one clear click away.
- No fake counters, fake testimonials, invented partner claims, guaranteed outcomes or misleading charity claims.

## OneWorldz.com Information Architecture

### 1. Hero — The World We Choose

Full cinematic hero using a dedicated OneWorldz production visual: humanity, children, community, landscape and future-facing optimism rather than technology-first imagery.

Headline: `OneWorldz`
Subline: `One Vision.`
Statement: `One World • One Mission`
Primary actions:
- Explore the Worldz
- Help People
- Learn

The hero must immediately link the emotional mission to the wider ecosystem.

### 2. The Mission — People Before Systems

Short visual section explaining the practical philosophy:
- Food and dignity
- Knowledge and opportunity
- Fair pathways and public information
- Community projects and humanitarian support
- Responsible technology and crypto education

No political-party positioning and no claim of governing authority. The emphasis is practical participation, transparency and people helping people.

### 3. Three Flagship OneWorldz Divisions

A premium three-destination presentation with unique visual identities and explicit hostnames.

#### Learn.OneWorldz.com
Purpose: knowledge centre.
Promise: `Knowledge Creates Power.`
Content pillars:
- Everyday digital literacy
- Blockchain and wallet safety
- AI literacy and responsible use
- Money, risk and scam awareness
- Community skills and practical learning
- Future expansion into structured courses and multilingual material

Visual direction: library + future classroom + human curiosity; luminous blue/violet; calm, intelligent, welcoming.

#### Law.OneWorldz.com
Purpose: public-information and pathway centre, never personalised legal advice.
Promise: `Understand the system. Know the pathway. Ask the right questions.`
Content pillars:
- Plain-language public information
- Consumer and debt-recovery pathways
- Rights-awareness navigation
- Government/process transparency resources
- Robin Hood Law / RobinWorldz relationship clearly separated from legal-advice claims
- Strong jurisdiction and professional-advice boundaries

Visual direction: civic architecture + human fairness + open pathways; deep green, gold and midnight; dignified rather than aggressive.

#### ImpactBased.OneWorldz.com
Purpose: purpose-driven project and impact launch board.
Promise: `Ideas with purpose. Projects with proof.`
Content pillars:
- Humanitarian and community projects
- Based.bid pathway where applicable
- Project discovery and progress
- Transparent support routes
- Real-world outcomes without exaggerated claims
- Clear separation between project visibility, donations and token activity

Visual direction: people creating visible change; warm gold, violet and sunrise tones; strong connection to Uganda and broader global projects without presenting a single country as the whole mission.

### 4. Worldz Explorer

A visual directory across the active ecosystem, grouped by purpose rather than a wall of logos:
- Crypto & chains
- Humanitarian & donations
- Learning & public information
- Community & legacy
- Command Centre / protected technology

Each card states what the destination actually does before the visitor clicks.

### 5. Human Impact

Feature real mission categories and approved stories/visuals. Reagan and the children can be an important anchor where approved, but OneWorldz remains broader than one fundraiser or organisation.

Routes include FoodWorldz and DonateWorldz with clear separation between information and payment action.

### 6. Stand As One — 2026–2030

Introduce the long-horizon OneWorldz humanitarian event concept without representing unconfirmed artists, sponsors, venues or commitments as secured.

Position it as a developing worldwide invitation around music, community, food, knowledge, infrastructure and practical humanitarian action.

### 7. Command Centre

A concise explanation of ZED, AUTO, G.R.A.C.E., RECAP and BASED.BID with human leadership separately labelled. No owner-only actions are exposed on OneWorldz.com. CryptoBotz remains the protected application destination.

### 8. Support and Action

Route visitors intentionally:
- See the need → FoodWorldz
- Choose a support purpose → DonateWorldz
- Learn → Learn.OneWorldz
- Public-information pathways → Law.OneWorldz
- Projects and impact → ImpactBased.OneWorldz

Payment streams remain separate and must retain their existing purpose labels.

### 9. Footer / Global Navigation

Persistent access to OneWorldz, CryptoWorldz, Learn, Law, ImpactBased, FoodWorldz, DonateWorldz and the Worldz Explorer.

Footer identity:
`Designed by JayJayTeamDev™`
`Helping the People Who Help People.`

## Shared Design System for the Four Sites

All four sites share:
- OneWorldz header family
- consistent navigation behavior
- consistent footer and ecosystem return route
- common spacing, accessibility and mobile rules
- shared typography hierarchy
- shared subtle globe/world motif

They do NOT share the same hero artwork or accent palette. Each destination must be visually identifiable at a glance.

## Technical / Deployment Gate

No production write is allowed until all of the following pass:

1. `oneworldz.com` resolves publicly and HTTPS loads.
2. Hostinger recognises OneWorldz.com as connected.
3. `learn.oneworldz.com`, `law.oneworldz.com`, `impactbased.oneworldz.com` each resolve publicly.
4. Exact Hostinger roots are independently proved.
5. A complete backup of the current OneWorldz.com live root is available.
6. The new OneWorldz V2 package builds and passes local verification.
7. Every referenced image exists, loads and has a mobile counterpart.
8. Internal and external links are checked.
9. DNS/mail records are not modified by deployment.
10. CryptoBotz and its MiniApp remain untouched.

Deployment order after the gate passes:
1. OneWorldz.com V2
2. Live proof of OneWorldz.com
3. Learn.OneWorldz.com
4. Law.OneWorldz.com
5. ImpactBased.OneWorldz.com
6. Cross-site navigation proof across all four

Any failed live proof stops that destination and triggers rollback. Successful existing destinations are not redeployed unnecessarily.

## Completion Definition

The release is complete only when:
- OneWorldz.com is visually and technically approved on desktop and mobile.
- Learn, Law and ImpactBased each have their own premium identity and are live-proved.
- OneWorldz links correctly into all three and all three link back to OneWorldz.
- no broken images or mismatched production imagery remain.
- protected CryptoBotz functions remain unchanged.
- FoodWorldz and DonateWorldz remain available from the global gateway when their own DNS/hosting gates pass.

Release working name: `oneworldz-head-domain-v2`.
