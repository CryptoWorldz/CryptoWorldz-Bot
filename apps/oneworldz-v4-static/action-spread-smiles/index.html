ACTION SPREAD SMILES ORGANISATION

Real children. Real needs. Real accountability.

Reagan Kauja and his community in Mayuge District, Uganda, care for around 60 orphaned and vulnerable children. This page keeps their whole story together.

60

THE FIRST COMMITMENT

Action is how a smile becomes sustainable.

Reagan's page is not a detachable campaign tile. It belongs on OneWorldz and every Worldz site because it tests the entire promise: can a connected community see a need, verify it, act, and show the outcome without exploiting the people it serves?

The children need daily stability as well as emergency help. Food, school, health, hygiene and shelter reinforce one another. A medical payment matters. So does the rent that keeps a safe roof over everyone the following month.

WHAT HELP MUST COVER

A whole life cannot be split into one emergency.

01

Medical care

Urgent examinations, treatment, medicine and transport when a child is unwell.

02

Food & hygiene

Daily meals, clean water, soap, sanitary supplies and durable food security.

03

Education

School fees, uniforms, books, stationery and consistent attendance.

04

Safe shelter

Stable rent, repairs, bedding and a home with proper safeguarding.

05

Local capacity

Reliable records, trusted suppliers and skills that remain in the community.

06

A future

Children known for their lives and potential—not reduced to a crisis photograph.

THE EVIDENCE PROMISE

Dignity first. Proof always.

Financial help should move through a visible campaign or agreed payment route. Before publication, the recipient, purpose, ownership and current availability of every link must be checked. After action, receipts and outcomes should be recorded without exposing children's private medical or identity information.

  • Current need and target confirmed by Reagan.
  • Campaign ownership and payout route shown clearly.
  • Funds received and spending recorded.
  • Outcome update published with consent and safeguarding.
