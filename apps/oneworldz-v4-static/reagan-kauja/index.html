REAGAN KAUJA · MAYUGE DISTRICT, UGANDA

The person on the ground has a name.

THE HUMAN CONNECTION

Reagan is not a footnote to the technology.

The network matters only when it helps a real person carry a real responsibility with less isolation and more support.

Reagan Kauja leads the day-to-day work connected to Action Spread Smiles Organisation in Mayuge District. The work includes care for around 60 orphaned and vulnerable children and the constant practical demands of food, health, school, hygiene and shelter.

This page is the permanent bridge. It should be reachable from every Worldz website, not buried inside one campaign. Current needs belong here. So do public evidence, completed actions and corrections when information changes.
