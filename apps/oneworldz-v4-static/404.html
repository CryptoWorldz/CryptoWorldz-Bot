THE HOPE CHEST · SINCE THE DREAM BEGAN

THE DREAM AT 23

Turn the machines of war into carriers of comfort.

The first picture was simple enough for a child to understand: former bombers crossing the Christmas sky—not with bombs, but with teddy bears floating down into children's arms.

That image became a direction. Peacekeeping without domination. A Purple Diamond Crew serving on the ground. A Hope Chest that connects a visible need to a visible response. Technology used to coordinate care, not to distance us from it.
