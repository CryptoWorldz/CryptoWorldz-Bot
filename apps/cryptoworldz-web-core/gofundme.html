HOW HELP WORKS

Donate. Share. Spread the word. Volunteer. Connect.

🌍

Areas of support

Food, clean water, medical care, safe shelter, clothing, hygiene, education, gardens and longer-term food security.

🌐

The world can help

Support can come from anywhere. A donation helps directly; a share can reach the next donor; an introduction can connect a project with food, medicine, transport, skills or funding.

💜

Ground-level action

Purple Diamond Crew and the wider OneWorldz network focus on practical help: identify a real need, verify who is responsible, support the work and show the result.

THE DISTRIBUTION PRINCIPLE

Need before popularity.

The Community Survival Fund is intended for verified humanitarian needs represented throughout the OneWorldz support network. Facebook pages help make needs visible and connect supporters; they are not automatic payment recipients. Distribution is coordinated through the fundraiser organiser to verified orphanage managers, carers and community projects according to genuine need.
