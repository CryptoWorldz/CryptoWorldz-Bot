# WorldzPad™ — Four Token Launch Master (#001–#004)

Status: **launch-preparation candidate**. Mainnet signing stays disabled until devnet, security, authority and jurisdiction checks pass and JayJayTeamDev explicitly signs.

## Shared architecture

ZED commands launches, profiles, missions, governance and status. AUTO handles transparent fee accounting, SOL holder rewards, LP routing and approved buy-only inputs. G.R.A.C.E. controls vesting, beneficiary records, permissions and approved social publishing. WorldzPad Flash Direct handles launch contracts and Real Value™ reporting.

Shared rules:
- Solana first.
- No bonding curve, no graduation, no private presale.
- 2.00% quote-side DEX trading-fee target; ordinary wallet transfers are not given a Worldz transfer tax.
- 1.00% of **every new token** is a separate Legacy Loyalty allocation for verified genuine legacy holders, regardless of other bonuses/rewards.
- Dev/LP/reserve/charity/locked system balances do not earn holder rewards.
- No guaranteed return, APY, future price, “100x”, fake volume, hidden wallets or fake liquidity.
- Whales can buy on the same public terms, but receive no private discount, extra reward rate or hidden allocation.
- Verified linked wallets are aggregated to reduce wallet-splitting abuse.
- Governance uses square-root weighting plus a per-profile power cap.

## Anti-whale / anti-greed design
- No private whale rounds or preferential price.
- Reward weight caps apply even when a whale owns more.
- Linked verified wallets aggregate before reward/governance calculations.
- Team/system wallets are tagged and excluded from holder rewards.
- Official WorldzPad UI can impose rate/size limits, while open DEX trading is never falsely described as identity-proof whale-proof.
- No wallet is punished merely for buying; the system changes **reward influence**, not ownership.
- Large liquidity providers remain welcome on the same disclosed rules.
