📜 DIPSHIT™ Pro Membership Terms

Last updated: 29 September 2026.

Service

DIPSHIT™ Pro is an optional paid digital-service membership for additional bot and Worldz tooling. Specific features may evolve as the service is developed.

Telegram Stars subscription

Digital subscriptions purchased inside Telegram are charged in Telegram Stars. The invoice shows the exact Stars price before purchase. A recurring Stars membership runs for 30 days per period and renews automatically unless cancelled.

You can use /cancelmembership to request that future Stars renewals stop. Cancellation does not remove access already paid for during the current period.

Optional external SOL month pass

The separate SOL option is a manual 30-day membership payment made outside Telegram's digital checkout. The bot displays the exact SOL amount and public recipient before payment. It does not automatically debit or control your wallet. After payment finalizes, you submit the public transaction signature for verification. Each signature can be claimed once.

A membership payment does not buy tokens, LP ownership, an investment interest, revenue share, profit rights or any promised financial return.

Price changes

Future prices may change. Any new price is shown before a new purchase or renewal setup. Telegram Stars are their own payment unit and are not represented here as being pegged to a fixed AUD or USD value.

Refunds and payment problems

Use /paysupport with the details of the issue. Stars refunds, where approved and technically available, are handled using Telegram's payment tools. Blockchain transfers cannot simply be reversed by the bot, so external SOL issues are reviewed individually.

Availability

The service may be interrupted for maintenance, security, Telegram availability, blockchain/network failures or other technical reasons. Paid access is not a guarantee that every feature will be continuously available.

Safety

Never provide a seed phrase, private key, password or payment-card details to the bot. You are responsible for checking the displayed recipient, amount and transaction details before approving an external wallet transaction.

Privacy

Use of the service is also subject to the DIPSHIT™ Privacy Policy.

Contact

Use /paysupport in @DipShitBossBot for subscription, cancellation, refund or billing support.
