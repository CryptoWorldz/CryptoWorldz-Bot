🔐 DIPSHIT™ Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026.

This policy explains the information used by the DIPSHIT™ Telegram bot, its support features and optional paid membership.

Information we use

We may process your Telegram user ID, username and basic profile details supplied by Telegram; membership status and expiry; Telegram Stars payment references; public Solana transaction signatures and amounts when you choose the external SOL month-pass option; and messages you deliberately send through bug-report or payment-support commands.

Why we use it

We use this information to operate the bot, verify and maintain paid access, prevent duplicate payment claims, provide support, investigate abuse or technical failures, process subscription cancellation/refund requests where applicable, and maintain necessary accounting or security records.

What we do not ask for

Never send a seed phrase, private key, password or payment-card details to DIPSHIT™. The bot does not need them to verify Telegram Stars or public Solana transactions.

Telegram Stars

Telegram processes Stars purchases and supplies the bot with payment status and a Telegram payment charge identifier. We store the minimum payment reference needed to activate access and support cancellation/refund handling.

External SOL payments

Solana transactions are public blockchain records. If you claim an external SOL month pass, the bot verifies the transaction against the configured public recipient and stores the transaction signature, amount and membership period so the same payment cannot be claimed twice.

Sharing

Information is not sold to advertisers. Data may be processed by infrastructure providers needed to operate the service, by Telegram for Telegram features and payments, and on the public Solana network when you independently make a SOL transaction. Support messages are visible to the Worldz operator handling your request.

Retention and deletion

Membership and payment records are kept only as long as reasonably needed for access control, fraud prevention, support, refunds, accounting and legal obligations. You may ask for deletion or correction of information that is not required to be retained.

Your choices

You can stop using the bot, cancel Stars auto-renewal with /cancelmembership, avoid the optional SOL route, and contact the operator with /paysupport for privacy or payment questions.

Contact

Use /paysupport in @DipShitBossBot and state that your request concerns privacy.
