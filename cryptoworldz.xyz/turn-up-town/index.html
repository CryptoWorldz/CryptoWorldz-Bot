The Turn Up Town rule

Show up → prove ownership → receive.

✨

1. Turn up

Choose a specific program and read its eligibility, dates, available rewards and terms.

🔐

2. Prove what’s yours

For a wallet program, sign a message with your own wallet. For community support, follow that program’s separate verification steps.

🎁

3. Receive

Eligible claims are checked against that program’s approved record before any gift, reward or donation is delivered.

Safety first: Signing a proof message should never require your seed phrase or a token transfer. A public wallet address alone does not prove ownership or guarantee a reward. Claiming is not live on this page yet.

WorldzLaunchPad™ • Build together, help together

A launch can give back.

Our approved fee-sharing model sets aside part of the fees Worldz actually controls for legacy communities and OneWorldz impact. It also rewards creators and the people introducing new participants. This is why we are building WorldzLaunchPad worldwide: real launches, published splits and receipts people can check.

🎨

51% Creator

For the people making and launching the project.

🤝

17% Referrer

For people helping others find and join the launch.

💜

15% Legacy Flywheel

Planned recurring support shared across ten verified legacy community vaults, with claims only when economical.

✦

8.5% WorldzLaunchPad

For building and operating the launch platform.

🌏

8.5% OneWorldz impact

For separately approved community impact, including future Help a Mate projects.

These percentages divide 100% of Worldz-controlled fees actually collected after outside venue deductions. They are a design target, not five additional charges or an active REVIVE payout. The planned 0.75% gross trading fee and direct-pool fee claim and router still need on-chain proof. RVIV's 200M token allocation is a separate plan.
